Operators first. Advisors by consequence.

Nine principals. Four continents. One idea: that the best strategic work is done by people who have been on the operator's side of the same problem — and can prove it.

01 · Our story

A boutique advisory built for the weight of real decisions.

GrowwithBA was founded in 2019 by a group of operators who had scaled, sold, and recapitalized companies across SaaS, commerce, fintech, and services — and were tired of hiring consultancies that hadn't.

We took the practice private on purpose. No pipeline pressure. No junior consultants dressed as partners. Just a small group of principals with skin in the operator's decision.

02 · Principles

The five rules we refuse to break.

01

Skin in the outcome.

We take equity, revenue share, or a bonus tied to what we ship — never pure hourly. If you don't win, we don't.

02

Principals in the room.

The partner you meet is the partner who does the work. No handoffs to associates. Ever.

03

Truth over comfort.

We won't tell you what you want to hear. Founders bring us in precisely because the room needs one person willing to say the quiet part.

04

Systems, not slides.

Every engagement leaves behind something you can operate — dashboards, cadences, hires — not a 90-page deck that dies on a Notion page.

05

Say no often.

We accept fewer than one in five engagements we're offered. If we're not the right fit, we say so within a week and often introduce someone who is.

+

Compound quietly.

Our best work isn't in press releases. It's in the businesses that quietly doubled while everyone else was still hiring their first CRO.
