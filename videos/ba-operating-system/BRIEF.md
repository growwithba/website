## Intent

Brand film for Bridging Associates (growwithba.com). The agency category is
commoditised — everyone sells the same list of posts, ads and SEO, and every
agency sends a report. The film reframes the choice: the problem was never
effort or deliverables, it is that nobody on the agency side owns the number.

BA's answer is structural, not promotional: five verticals, each with a named
lead who owns their numbers, a scorecard filled every Friday, and a rule that a
red status two weeks running means the lead arrives with a proposed fix. BA runs
its own brands on the same system, so it is an operator rather than an advisor.

Concept explainer. Diagram- and mechanism-led. No talking head, no site capture.
Confident and operational, not hypey.

## Notes

- No BA brand palette exists in the available context (the colours in the
  growwithba/website repo belong to PrimeDirectory, an unrelated directory
  product). Take the frame preset's own design system, and deliberately NOT
  purple — the Think14 film already owns purple and the two must not read as
  one brand.
- The five verticals, exact names and order: SMM / CREATIVE · DIGITAL ·
  BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT · ECOMMERCE · GROWTH + AI.
- Mechanism facts that are safe to state: leads own their numbers; scorecard
  every Friday 6 PM; green / amber / red status; red two weeks running means
  the lead calls a meeting and brings a proposed fix, not just the problem;
  BA runs its own brands on the same system.
- DO NOT invent revenue, ROAS, client counts, growth percentages or any
  result metric — no verified client figures are available, and a fabricated
  number in a brand film is a liability. Structure and mechanism carry this
  film; there is no proof-by-numbers beat.
- Do not name any client, any team member, or any internal file name.
- "Bridging Associates" is the full name; BA is the short form. URL is
  growwithba.com.
- Tone: short sentences, hard full stops. No "unlock", no "seamless", no
  "we are passionate about".
