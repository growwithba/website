· Common questions

Before you write.

What size company do you typically work with?

Most partners are between $5M and $250M in revenue. Below that we sometimes advise informally; above that we usually work with an operating unit or portfolio company.

Do you work with venture-backed founders?

Often — but not exclusively. Roughly half our partners are VC-backed, a third are founder-owned and profitable, and the rest are portfolio companies of PE or family offices.

How long is a typical engagement?

Most start with a 10–14 week structured engagement, then continue in a retained governance mode. The average total partnership runs 18–36 months.

Do you take equity in lieu of fees?

Sometimes. On the right engagement we structure some or all of our compensation as equity, revenue share, or exit-linked bonus. We prefer skin in the outcome.

What if we're pre-revenue?

We take on a small number of pre-revenue partners each year — usually second-time founders with a clean thesis and enough runway to work through a structured 12-week engagement.
